Every question changes something we would actually do — which is why we ask it, and why there are only nine. You will have a written answer within one business day, either way.

What is a new customer worth in the first year? *
Below $2,000 the arithmetic of a paid plan never works, and we would be wasting your month.
How many sales calls a week could you actually take? *
This decides which plan we would ever recommend on day 30. Booking more calls than you can hold is how these go wrong.
Has anyone outside your network ever bought from you? *
Cold email tests distribution, not whether the offer works. If strangers have never bought, the trial would be testing the wrong thing.
Could you take a booked meeting within five business days? *
Interested prospects go cold faster than anything else in this process. A closed calendar wastes the best replies we get you.
